Marketplace Rules

IndustrCons Market-də etibarlı mühit qorumaq üçün bütün istifadəçilər aşağıdakı qaydalara riayət etməlidir:

  • No fake listings
  • No misleading information
  • No stolen goods
  • No fake documents
  • No phishing
  • No payment fraud
  • No impersonation
  • No spam
  • No duplicate listings
  • No illegal products
  • No unauthorized property listings
  • No manipulation of prices
  • No fraudulent reviews
⚠️
IndustrCons may suspend or permanently restrict accounts that violate marketplace rules.
